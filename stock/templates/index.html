How This LSTM Stock Predictor Works (Engineering Architecture)

A clear breakdown of the data science and deep learning workflow.

01

Chronological Splitting

Time-series data cannot be randomly shuffled because future prices depend on past sequence order. We allocate the first 80% of dates for training and the most recent 20% strictly for testing.

02

No Data Leakage

The MinMaxScaler is fitted only on the training dataset. The test set and future inputs are transformed using the train-scaler parameters, strictly preventing future information leakage.

03

60-Day Lookback Window

Every input sequence contains 60 consecutive trading days $X_t = [P_{t-60}, \dots, P_{t-1}]$ to predict the next day's price $y_t = P_t$. This captures momentum, quarterly earnings trends, and volatility.

04

2-Layer Stacked LSTM

Two LSTM layers (50 units each) with Dropout (20%) prevent overfitting. Memory cells and forget gates retain long-term market trends while filtering transient day-to-day noise.
